FAQ

Questions, answered

What do I need to give you?

Your expertise, in whatever form is easiest: notes, a call, a voice memo, a post you liked. I find the angle, plan the piece and design it.

Do you write the content too?

Strategy and planning are part of the retainer. We decide together what each piece should say and why, and I shape it into slides and visuals.

Can you work with my existing brand?

Yes. If you already have colours and fonts, I build on them. If you don't, the Brand System Setup creates them first.

How much does it cost?

Every brand is at a different stage, so there are no flat prices. On the free strategy call you get an exact quote, with no pressure and no obligation.

Do you make animated content?

Yes. Animated infographics and GIFs are a big part of my work, alongside static carousels, covers and infographics.

Who posts the content?

You do. Everything arrives ready to post, so you stay in control of what goes live and when.
